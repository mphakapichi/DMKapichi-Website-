About Me

Cybersecurity Specialist. Infrastructure Architect. Problem Solver.

I am Dickson Mervyn Kapichi — a cybersecurity specialist and infrastructure architect with over 6 years of hands-on experience turning complex security challenges into elegant, resilient solutions.

I design and build secure systems that are not just functional, but genuinely impactful — from penetration testing and vulnerability assessments to enterprise infrastructure hardening and cloud security architecture. I combine technical depth with strategic thinking, having delivered solutions for government bodies, NGOs, startups, and enterprises across Malawi.

I hold a BSc in Management Information Systems and am actively pursuing advanced certifications in offensive security (CPTS, OSCP). My expertise spans Active Directory administration, Linux/Windows hardening, network security, digital forensics, and cloud infrastructure.
